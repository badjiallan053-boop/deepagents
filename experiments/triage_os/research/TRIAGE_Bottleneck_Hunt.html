02Method

Top-down chokepoints (MOFCOM export controls, DoD DPA Title III/DLA awards, NWF/EXIM finance, NDAA sourcing rules) → small listed firms that hold the scarce asset, permit or qualification → evidence graded A (government document), B (company primary filing) or C (company claim or trade press, not re-verified) → score.

Score (0–5) = 25% chokepoint share + 20% revenue sensitivity + 15% size + 15% evidence grade + 10% liquidity + 15% not-crowded. The weights are judgement. Contrarian check: 12-month return >200%, top 20% of 52-week range, 3-month return >60%. Quant Validation: every name is VETOED as recommendation-grade because no backtested KEPT signal underlies it, so all are paper WATCH calls.

The dated catalyst for the whole metals cluster: MOFCOM Announcement 72/2025 suspends the US-specific ban on gallium, germanium, antimony and superhard materials (Ann. 46/2024 Art. 2) only until 27 Nov 2026. Art. 1 (no dual-use items to US military end users) stays in force. Source: mofcom.gov.cn/zcfb/blgg/gg/202…4f36dc5.html

1. United States Antimony (UAMY)

  • Chain: US DoD National Defense Stockpile → antimony metal ingots → USAC, operator of North America's only two antimony smelters
  • Verified fact: DLA sole-source 5-yr IDIQ SP8000-25-D-0007, max $245m (no obligation beyond $5k minimum); first delivery order ~$10m (315,000 lb). China's US antimony ban (MOFCOM Ann. 46/2024 Art. 2) is suspended only until 27 Nov 2026 (Ann. 72/2025).
  • Catalyst: 2026-11-27: MOFCOM suspension of the US-specific antimony/gallium/germanium ban expires unless renewed
  • Key risks: IDIQ max is not revenue; feed-ore sourcing (Mexico/Bolivia); China renews the suspension → price normalises. Dilution risk: MEDIUM.
  • Kill condition: MOFCOM renews the suspension AND antimony price (Rotterdam) falls >30% from 23 Oct 2026; or no second DLA delivery order by 30 Jun 2027
  • Contrarian: Not crowded on price. Quant Validation: VETO for recommendation-grade (no backtested KEPT signal; WATCH only).
